# PACE Product Language

PACE helps active people meet through a shared lifestyle. A connection may become an activity partnership, a romantic relationship, or both; the product does not force that intent before people meet.

## Language

**Connection**:
A mutual relationship created after two members express genuine interest in each other.
_Avoid_: Click, win, result

**Match**:
The user-facing name for a newly created Connection or the list containing those Connections.
_Avoid_: Shared momentum, training result

**Active person**:
A member for whom movement or sport is a meaningful part of everyday life, regardless of competitive level.
_Avoid_: Athlete, serious trainer

**Shared activity**:
A sport or active interest that gives two members useful context for meeting and spending time together.
_Avoid_: Performance requirement, competition metric

**Activity partner**:
Someone a member may meet for a shared activity without implying or excluding romantic interest.
_Avoid_: Training-only partner

**Romantic connection**:
A Connection where both members choose to explore a relationship beyond shared activities.
_Avoid_: Date by default
